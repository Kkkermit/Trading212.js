<Aside type="caution" title="Deprecated">
	Trading 212 has deprecated the pie endpoints. They still work, but will not be updated and may be removed.
</Aside>

```ts
const [first] = await t212.pies.list();
if (typeof first?.id === "number") console.log(await t212.pies.get(first.id));
```

| Method                 | Does                                    | Permission   | Limit    |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------ | -------- |
| `pies.list()`          | Every pie, with its progress            | `pies:read`  | 1 / 30 s |
| `pies.get(id)`         | One pie with its instruments            | `pies:read`  | 1 / 5 s  |
| `pies.create(pie)`     | Makes a pie                             | `pies:write` | 1 / 5 s  |
| `pies.update(id, pie)` | Changes a pie's settings or instruments | `pies:write` | 1 / 5 s  |
| `pies.duplicate(id)`   | Copies a pie, optionally renamed        | `pies:write` | 1 / 5 s  |
| `pies.delete(id)`      | Deletes a pie                           | `pies:write` | 1 / 5 s  |

The first call to each logs a warning. Changing a pie counts as trading, so a live client needs `trading: "enabled"`,
and a change is never retried.
